Dumbo Jump Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026 (revised)

한국어

Dumbo Jump does not collect personal information. There is no account and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email, phone number, or location. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What is stored on your device

The game has to remember where you left off. The following is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Stars earned per stage, and which stages are unlocked
  • Skill unlock progress
  • Settings (haptics, sound effects, music)
  • Your best Endless Climb score

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it yourself from the in-game settings.

Advertising

Dumbo Jump shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use device information to select and deliver those ads, and that processing is governed by Google's own policies.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Why we ask about tracking

On iOS, the app asks whether you allow tracking the first time you open it. If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used to choose more relevant ads.

Saying no changes nothing about the game. Ads still appear and no feature is locked — the ads are simply not personalised.

You can change your answer at any time.

  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking
  • In the EEA and the UK, an ad consent form appears at first launch.
  • In some US states, you can revisit the choice from Settings › Privacy options inside the game.

Sharing with third parties

Because the developer collects nothing, there is nothing to share or sell. The advertising described above is the whole picture.

Analytics

None. The app contains no analytics or usage-tracking library.
