Common questions

My progress is gone. Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app or switching devices does not carry it over — there is no account, so the developer has no copy either.

I want to see the tutorial again. Open Settings and tap "Replay tutorial".

There is no sound. Sound effects and music are separate toggles in Settings. Also check whether your device is on silent.

Privacy
